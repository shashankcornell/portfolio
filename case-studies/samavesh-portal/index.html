01

Why a portal

Samavesh had years of discourses and no good place to keep them.

Each Samavesh discourse is a recorded conversation on one policy idea. The record of that work lived in a spreadsheet of past sessions and in scattered posts. A reader could not browse it by topic or by speaker, and the team could not publish without someone technical in the loop.

I wanted one place where a recording becomes a paper, the paper is credited to everyone who shaped it, and a reader can find it from any of those angles.

03

How content is tagged

Every piece on the portal carries the same set of tags: the policy domain it belongs to, its format, and each person who worked on it, with their role. Those tags are what tie the whole site together. Each one leads to a page of its own.

FormatDiscourseFormat page, with all discourses

DiscussantsWho respondedEach discussant's profile
EditorWho edited itThe editor's profile

The same person can be a presenter on one piece and an editor on another. So people are stored once, as profiles, and each piece links to them with a role: author, presenter, discussant, contributor or editor. Formats are not fixed either. The team can add a new one, such as a podcast or an op-ed, without code.

05

The front page

The homepage should feel varied and current, without someone rearranging it every week. A short rule chooses what goes in the grid.

Step 1

Three of every format

The newest three pieces of each format get a place, so no format disappears.

Step 2

Fill with the newest

The remaining slots go to the most recent work, whatever its format.

Step 3

Shuffle

The grid is shuffled on each visit, so formats mix and returning readers see a fresh page.

Manual

When the editors want a particular front page, for a summit or a new Gazette, they switch to manual mode and pin exactly what goes where, along with a featured story.

07

For editors

An admin CMS lets the team run the site without a developer.

Write and creditA rich-text editor. Credit each person with their role, tag the topic, and add audio, video or an embed.Draft→Published→Archived
Shape the siteEdit the Think, Meet, Act, Careers and policy pages, post news with social embeds, build each Gazette edition, and set the front page.
Keep it tidyImages are cropped on upload. When a record is deleted, its images go too, unless another page still uses them.

Trust

A site that hosts public debate has to be hard to deface. Logins are limited to ten attempts every fifteen minutes and passwords are stored hashed. Changing content needs a signed-in session. Inputs are validated, published text is cleaned against a list of safe tags, and uploads are limited to images under 5 MB.

08

Outcome

I built the portal between April and June 2026. A one-time import brought in 88 past discourses from August 2020 to February 2022, creating presenter profiles and topic tags as it went. The data model grew from a simple publishing schema into one that also covers the journal, news, configurable pages and the front page, across four migrations.
