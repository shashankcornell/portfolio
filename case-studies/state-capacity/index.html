01

The question

Does India have enough people in government to deliver what it plans?

At the first ever meeting of the Prime Minister with the chief secretaries of every state, one problem stood out. The country does not have enough capacity in government. There are two ways to close that gap: train the people already in service, or hire more people where posts are empty or new ones are needed.

Lever 1

Train the existing workforce

Build the skills of the people already in service.
Lever 2 · this project

Hire where there are gaps

Fill vacant posts and add posts where they are needed. That first needs to know where the vacancies are.

No one had a national count of vacant posts in urban departments. Hiring could not be planned without one.

02

The survey

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs ran the first ever survey of its kind. Forms went to urban departments across the country. Where a department was online, it got a digital form. Where it was not, a paper form was sent.

04

The dashboard

I built the dashboard in Looker Studio. It answers one question at three levels: how much capacity there is, and where it falls short.

CountryThe national picturePosts, filled and vacant, for the whole country at a glance.
StateWhere the gaps areEach state side by side, so the largest shortfalls stand out.
Department and postWhat to hire forThe kinds of posts that sit empty, which is what a hiring plan needs.

Filters for state, department and post let a reader move from the country down to a single kind of post without leaving the page.

05

Outcome

The findings were reported to the Prime Minister's Office. For the first time, the government had a national view of vacant posts in urban departments, built from the departments' own responses.
