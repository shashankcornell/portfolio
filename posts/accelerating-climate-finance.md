During India's G20 Presidency in 2023 I served on the Urban20 (U20) Technical Secretariat, on deputation from the National Institute of Urban Affairs. One of the things we produced was India's official white paper, *Accelerating Climate Finance in G20 Cities*.

I co-authored it, coordinating inputs from an 8-member global task force, 3 advisors and 57 city stakeholders. It was published on the global G20 portal as India's 2023 submission.

**Citation.** Mishra, S. (co-author, 2023). *Accelerating Climate Finance in G20 Cities*. Urban20 white paper, India's G20 Presidency.
