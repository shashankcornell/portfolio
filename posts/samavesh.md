Samavesh is an independent Indian policy think tank that tests emerging policy ideas through convenings and moderated public discourse. I co-founded it in February 2020 and have run it since.

We built an 18-member team and designed and ran three national policy summits with leading policy schools and global think tanks. Over 150+ moderated discussions we curated 280+ policy ideas, and developed the strongest 13 into edited articles for India's first discourse-led policy journal.

To keep those discussions useful, we wrote and enforced guidelines for respectful, evidence-based and non-discriminatory participation. Samavesh raised INR 3,000,000+ from a government grant and individual donors, and built partnerships across 15 Indian and international institutions.

I also designed and built its editorial platform, the Samavesh Portal, which is written up under Products.
